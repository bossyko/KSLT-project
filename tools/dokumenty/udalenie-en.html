Edition of 3 November 2026.

This page opens without signing in. Its address is given in Google Play Console («Data safety» → «Account deletion») and in App Store Connect.

01 Who deletes the data

The Public Foundation «Kyrgyzstan Community of Tennis Enthusiasts» (reg. No. 197575-3301-ОФ, Bishkek, Chuy Avenue 127, apt. 26), owner of the tennis.kg website and of the «KSLT Tennis» applications for Android and iOS.

Questions: kslt.kyrgyzstan@gmail.com

02 How to delete your account

2.1. In the app: Profile → Settings → «Delete account» → confirm the deletion.

2.2. On the website: sign in to your account area → Settings → «Delete account» and confirm in the dialog.

2.3. Without signing in: write to kslt.kyrgyzstan@gmail.com from the address registered to the account, subject «Account deletion». We will delete the account within 7 working days and confirm it.

03 What happens immediately

3.1. The profile stops being visible to other users and disappears from public lists at once.

3.2. Signing in is blocked and mailings stop.

3.3. For thirty days the account can be restored: just sign in and we will offer to bring it back. Until that period ends, nothing is erased permanently.

04 What is deleted after 30 days

4.1. The sign-in account — first and last name in the profile, e-mail, date of birth, gender, phone, photo, password and the Google and Telegram links.

4.2. Notification tokens and technical sign-in data.

4.3. Loyalty points and issued discounts.

4.4. Notification settings and all consents.

05 What remains and why

5.1. The player card with match results and points earned remains. Those results belong to the tournaments you played in: they are part of the history of other people's matches, draws and the rating, and erasing them would change the outcome of past competitions. In the tournament history they are kept anonymised or with first and last name only in tournament protocols (archival and statistical purposes, clause 2 part 2 Article 83 of the Digital Code of the KR).

5.2. The card is no longer linked to the account — it is marked «Account deleted».

5.3. Records of acceptance of the User Agreement and of consents — 3 years after deletion (part 4 Article 114 of the Digital Code of the KR).

5.4. Payment details — for the period established by accounting legislation.

5.5. Last name, first name and e-mail remain in the internal log of deleted accounts, so that the club can answer if you come back with a question about your account.

06 How to restore within 30 days

6.1. Sign in the same way you did before.

6.2. We will offer to restore the account — confirm, and everything comes back: rating, points and settings.

6.3. After thirty days restoration is not possible.

07 Deleting part of your data instead

7.1. Individual items — photo, phone, social media links — can be removed in your account area without deleting the account.

7.2. The same place switches off showing your phone and Telegram to other players.
