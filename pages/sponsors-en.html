For sponsors

Your brand next to the people who make the decisions

KSLT is the largest amateur tennis community in Kyrgyzstan. Our members include the heads of the country’s leading companies.

Audience

Who you reach

350+KSLT members with paid membership
900+people in the community chats
120+rated tournaments since 2021
30+international tournaments

Plus the social reach: followers on Instagram, members on Telegram. YouTube carries our live tournament broadcasts.

Profile

This is not just “a lot of people”

Tennis is not a cheap sport, and that shapes who plays it. Our members pay membership fees, entry fees, court rent and coaching — regularly, for years.

Adults who can spend

An amateur in KSLT means a player aged 25 and over. These are established people with their own income, not a student audience.

Executives and business owners

KSLT members include representatives and heads of Kyrgyzstan’s leading organisations and companies. Your brand is seen by the people who decide on purchasing and partnerships themselves.

Loyal and long-term

People join the community for years: they play tournaments, follow the ranking, talk in the chats every day. This is not the one-off reach of a billboard.

An international stage

Players come to our tournaments from Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Russia. Your brand travels beyond Bishkek at no extra cost.
