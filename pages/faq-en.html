Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most popular questions about KSLT, membership, tournaments, and the ranking system

General

KSLT (Kyrgyzstan Social Lawn Tennis) is the first amateur tennis community in Kyrgyzstan. We unite players of all levels: from beginners to advanced amateurs. We organize tournaments, maintain rankings, help find playing partners, and develop tennis culture in the country.
Register on the website, fill out your profile, and choose a membership plan. After paying the membership fee, you'll get access to the ranking system, be able to participate in tournaments, and enjoy all community benefits.
No! KSLT accepts players of all levels. We have categories ranging from Tour (beginners) to Pro-Masters (advanced). You'll play against opponents at your skill level, making every match exciting and competitive.
Currently, KSLT is based in Bishkek. We plan to expand to other cities in Kyrgyzstan — Osh, Karakol, Jalal-Abad. Stay tuned for updates!

Membership & Pricing

We offer several membership plans — from basic to premium. The basic plan includes ranking participation and tournament schedule access. The premium plan unlocks priority tournament registration, personal statistics, and other perks. See the pricing page for details.
Payment is available via Mbank, bank cards, and cash. After payment, your membership is activated automatically. You'll receive confirmation via email and in your personal dashboard.
Yes, you can pause your membership for up to 3 months (e.g., during vacation or for health reasons). Contact support to do so. Your ranking points are preserved during the pause.

Tournaments

Go to the tournaments page, select the tournament you're interested in, and click "Register". You need to be a registered KSLT member with an active membership. Spots are limited, so we recommend registering early.
KSLT hosts tournaments in 6 categories: Pro-Masters (highest level), Masters, Challenger, Futures, Tour (beginners), and Friendly (casual). Each category has its own ranking bracket and point system.
Notify the organizers at least 24 hours before the match. No-shows (WO — walkover) result in penalty points in the ranking. Repeated no-shows may lead to disqualification from future tournaments.
Pro-Masters and Masters categories feature sponsor prizes — sports equipment, gift certificates, free membership. In other categories, the main rewards are ranking points, medals, and community recognition.

Rankings & Categories

The KSLT ranking is based on a points system. Points are awarded for tournament and match wins. The number of points depends on the tournament category, stage (finals award more than round of 16), and the opponent's ranking. Rankings are updated after each tournament.
Category transitions happen automatically based on accumulated ranking points and results. Top players in a lower category receive an invitation to the next one. You can also apply for promotion if you believe your level is above your current category.
Yes, ranking points have an "expiration date". Results older than 12 months gradually lose weight. This encourages regular tournament participation and keeps the rankings current.

Courts & Play

Matches take place at partner tennis courts in Bishkek. The list of available courts and schedules can be found on the website. For tournaments, we book courts in advance; for friendly matches, players arrange courts themselves.
We're working on an online court booking system. Currently, you can contact the administration for booking assistance or use court contacts on our website.
On the rankings page, you can see players' online status. Use the "Challenge" button next to a player's name. You can also post in our Telegram chat — there are always people looking for a game.
